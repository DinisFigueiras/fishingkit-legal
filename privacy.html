This summary provides key points from our Privacy Notice. You can find out more details by using the table of contents below.

1. WHAT INFORMATION DO WE COLLECT?

Information stored only on your device

In Short: your tackle box never reaches us.

The rigs you create (name, fishing type, the gear list and which pieces you ticked), the list of fishing types you keep, the outings you note (the day, the rig it used, the place you pinned with its coordinates and its name, and the optional bait, species, number of fish, biggest size, hours and notes) and the wishes you save (name, price, web address, picture and whether you bought it) are stored locally on your device. Wish pictures are kept as copies in the app’s own folder on your device. We have no access to any of it. If you delete a wish, its picture is deleted too; if you uninstall the app or use Delete all my data, everything is removed from your device.

The map and the places you pin

In Short: the map and the place name come from your phone’s own services, not from us.

  • The map. To pick a place, the app shows Apple Maps. Apple receives the requests needed to draw the map, including the area you are looking at, under Apple’s privacy policy. We do not receive them.
  • Your location. Only when you tap Use my location, the app asks your phone for permission to use your location while the app is open. It reads your position once, to centre the map on you, and does not keep it or follow you. Only the pin you confirm is saved, on your device. You can refuse or withdraw this permission at any time in your device settings, and you can still move the map by hand.
  • The name of the place. When you tap Use this place, the app asks your phone’s place-lookup service (Apple’s, on an iPhone) for a name for the pinned coordinates, such as a river or a town. The coordinates go to that service, not to us. If it finds nothing, or you are offline, the pin is kept without a name.
  • Open in Maps. When you tap it, the app hands the pin to the Maps app on your phone.

Requests to the websites of the links you save

In Short: your phone contacts the shop, not us.

  • Reading the page. Only when you tap Read the link on a wish, the app requests the web page you entered, directly from your phone, to look for its product picture, name and price, which it offers to fill in for you. The reading is done on your phone; the page is not sent anywhere else. This is the same request a browser makes when you open the link.
  • The shop’s page inside the app. Some shops do not answer plain requests. For those, you can tap Open the shop’s page: the page opens in a browser inside the app, just as it would in your own browser, and the app reads the product details it shows. Anything you do on that page (including cookies the shop sets while it is open) is between you and the shop. We do not receive it.
  • Keeping the picture. The app then downloads that picture and keeps a copy on your device, so your wish list also shows it offline. It is never copied to our servers because we have none.
  • The website you contact receives the information any web request carries, such as your IP address and your device and app type, and handles it under its own privacy policy. We do not receive or see these requests.

Purchases

In Short: Pro is bought through Apple or Google, and we never see your payment details.

FishingKit Pro is a one-time purchase made through the Apple App Store (or Google Play). Apple or Google processes the payment under their own policies; we never receive your name, card or Apple ID. To tell the app that Pro is yours, and to bring it back if you reinstall or change phone, the app uses RevenueCat, a purchase service. RevenueCat receives an anonymous identifier created for the app, and your purchase status, and it handles them under its own privacy policy. This happens only when you open the Pro sheet, buy or restore, or when the app confirms your purchase.

Backups you create

When you choose Export a backup, the app writes a file containing your rigs, fishing types, outings (including the places you pinned) and wishes (with the web addresses of their pictures, but not the picture files kept on your device) and opens your device’s share sheet. The file goes only where you choose to save or send it, and anyone who has it can read it. When you choose Restore from a backup, the app reads the file you select on your device. Nothing is sent to us.

Device permissions

Location (while using the app). Only for Use my location, as described above. FishingKit does not ask for access to your camera, photo library, contacts or microphone, and it does not use an advertising identifier. Saving and picking backup files use your device’s own file and share screens.

Support requests

If you email us, we receive your email address and what you write, and we use them only to reply to you.

Sensitive information

We do not process sensitive personal information.

  • Legitimate interests. To answer your support requests and keep the Services working.

The data stored on your phone is under your control and is not processed by us.
