Help and support

Help for FishingKit, the app for your rigs, your outings and your wish list.

FishingKit keeps each rig (rod, reel, line and gear) as a checklist, remembers where and how each outing went, and holds a wish list of the gear you want to buy. Everything stays on your phone.

Contact us

Something not working, or an idea to make FishingKit better? Write to us. We usually reply within 2 business days. Please include your phone model, the iOS or Android version and a short description of the problem.

Questions people ask

Where are my rigs and outings stored?

Only on your phone. FishingKit has no accounts and no cloud sync. If you lose or reset your phone, your data is lost unless you made a backup. Go to Settings → Export a backup now and then, and keep the file somewhere safe (Files, iCloud Drive, email).

How do I move everything to a new phone?

On the old phone use Settings → Export a backup and save the file. On the new phone install FishingKit, open Settings → Restore from a backup and pick the file. Restoring adds what is missing and never changes what is already on the phone. The free plan restores up to 3 rigs; FishingKit Pro restores them all.

What do Free and Pro include?

Free keeps up to 3 rigs and the list of your outings (the day, the rig and the pin on the map). FishingKit Pro is a single payment, not a subscription, and gives you unlimited rigs, the “what worked” notes on each outing (bait, species, fish caught, size, hours and notes) and the wish list. What you already saved is never locked, even on the free plan.

How do I restore my Pro purchase?

Open Settings, tap About Pro and choose Restore purchase while signed in to the same Apple ID (or Google account) you used to buy. Pro is bought once and is yours to keep.

How does the map pin work?

On a new outing, tap Where, move the map until the bobber is where you fished and tap Use this place. Use my location is a shortcut that asks for your location once, to centre the map. The pin and its name stay on your phone: they are never sent to us and never appear in anything you share. Open in Maps hands the pin to the Maps app.

The place has no name

The name comes from your phone’s own place lookup, which needs a connection. If it finds nothing, the pin is kept without a name and still works.

“Read the link” did not find my gear

Some shops turn plain requests away. Tap Open the shop’s page to see the page inside FishingKit, open the product, then tap Use this page. You can always type the name and the price by hand. Prices are in euros.

I deleted a rig by mistake

Right after deleting, the message at the bottom has an Undo button. Past that moment a rig can only come back from a backup. Outings keep the name of a deleted rig and show it as “Rig removed”.

How do I delete my data?

Open Settings → Delete all my data to remove every rig, outing, wish, fishing type and wish picture from your phone. There is nothing to delete on our side because there are no accounts and we hold no copy.
